The dashboard pulls interviews happening today, follow-ups you scheduled, and suggestions for things that have gone quiet. Dismiss one and it stays gone for two weeks.

The whole pipeline

Applications in a table or kanban, interview rounds grouped by company, networking contacts, and saved jobs you can turn into an application in one click.

What is actually converting

Insights compares referral against everything else, source by source, and whether a contact at the company changes the outcome. Every figure shows its sample size.

Role Fit, with a quota

Paste a posting. The read uses the background you saved and is sent to Anthropic for that request. Free accounts get a few reads a month. Paid accounts get a much higher cap, and there is a hard daily ceiling for everyone.

Your data, exportable

JSON export and import are per account. Deleting the account deletes the applications, notes, contacts, and profile that belong to it.

Sign in without a password
